What is an On-Grid solar system?+

An On-Grid system is connected to the electricity grid. Solar power can help meet your property's daytime electricity needs; actual savings depend on usage, system design and local utility rules.

What is a Hybrid solar system?+

A Hybrid system combines solar generation with grid power and can include battery backup. The system is configured to match your needs and site.

Does solar work during cloudy or rainy weather?+

Panels can still generate electricity in cloudy conditions, but output is generally lower than in direct sunlight. Your final system estimate should account for local conditions and shade.

How much electricity can a solar system generate?+

Generation varies with system size, location, roof direction, shade, weather and equipment. We assess your property before recommending a configuration.

How much roof space is required?+

The space depends on the system capacity and roof conditions. A site assessment is the best way to understand what can fit safely on your property.

What maintenance does a solar system need?+

Keeping panels clear of heavy dust and having the system inspected when needed can help. The right maintenance schedule depends on the equipment and site.

Does S.K Bright provide installation and service?+

Yes. S.K Bright Solar Energy provides solar installation, inspection, maintenance, repair and service support.

Which areas does S.K Bright serve?+

Our team is based in Ramnagar, Varanasi and serves customers across Uttar Pradesh.
